Product modules

Front office to final accounts — each module works on its own and shares one live database.

Front Office

Reservations, check-in / out, room chart, guest folio and night audit.

Restaurant & Bar

Table & room KOTs, waiter-wise orders, split billing and thermal prints.

Kitchen Display

Live KOT screen for the kitchen with delay alerts and housekeeping status.

Housekeeping

Room status, cleaning, complaints and maintenance tracked in real time.

Retail & POS

Outlet invoicing, split payments, B.T.C. and room-credit settlements.

Store & Inventory

Stock items, vendors, purchase & issue, min/max levels and valuation.

Accounts

Account groups, ledgers, account summary and outstanding — Tally-style.

Reports & MIS

Department-wise reports for management — sales, tax, occupancy and more.

SMS Service

Send booking, folio and greeting messages straight to guests from the app.
